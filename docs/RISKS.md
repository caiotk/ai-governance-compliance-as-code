## Categories

- **security** — Adversarial and cyber risks to the model, its data and its surroundings
- **privacy** — Personal data and confidentiality
- **safety** — Harmful, unsafe or inaccurate output
- **fairness** — Bias, discrimination and unequal treatment
- **transparency** — People not knowing AI is involved or why it decided
- **agentic** — Risks specific to agents that plan, call tools and act
- **operational** — Availability, resilience, cost and change
- **supply-chain** — Third-party models, data, components and services
- **compliance** — Regulatory, contractual and intellectual-property exposure

### RSK-34 — Unvetted model, dataset or component

A model, dataset, plugin, MCP server or library is used without assessment, is not what it claims to be, or carries a licence you cannot meet.

- **Category:** supply-chain · **Severity:** high · **Verification:** primary
- **Applies when:** every system
- **Controls:** AIG-06 Approved models and AI supply chain, AIG-04 Data governance, quality and provenance, AIG-14 Pre-deployment testing, evaluation and red-teaming
- **OWASP GenAI Security Project — LLM Top 10 (2026) and Top 10 for Agentic Applications:** LLM04:2026 Supply Chain; ASI04 Agentic Supply Chain Vulnerabilities

- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 + Generative AI Profile:** AI 600-1 2.12 Value Chain and Component Integration

### RSK-35 — Outsourcing concentration and lost regulator access

A material service provider cannot be exited, audited or reached by the regulator; concentration in one vendor becomes a prudential issue.
