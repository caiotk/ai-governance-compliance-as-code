Research date: 2026-10-13. Scope: BR, CL, AR, PT, ES, US, AU, NZ, JP, SG + EU layer + international standards.

Verification labels
- **primary** = read on the issuer's own page/PDF/API during this research (URL given is the one actually read, or the canonical issuer URL for the same document).
- **secondary** = well-known fact not re-read today on the issuer's page (issuer site blocked or not reachable); treat as "likely correct, confirm before publishing".
- **UNVERIFIED** = could not read; may be wrong; do not cite without checking.

## 13. High-risk triggers for AI in energy (derived from the instruments above)

1. **AI as safety component in supply of gas, heating or electricity** → EU AI Act Annex III(2) high-risk (primary) — applies to PT/ES (and any vendor selling into EU). Analogous "safety-critical" flags: NERC CIP-002 BES Cyber System categorisation (US), Japan ESP Act 特定重要設備 (control systems ≥500 MW etc.), Singapore CII designation, Chile OIV designation, Australia critical electricity/gas/liquid-fuel asset.
2. **AI deployed on/with OT or control-centre systems** → NERC CIP-005/-007/-010/-012/-015 (US); SG CCoP 2026 §10 OT and §3.9 cloud restriction (cloud-hosted AI touching CII needs risk assessment first); Brazil ONS ARCiber controls; Japan METI technical-standard cyber requirements (2016/2022); AU CIRMP s8A(2)(c) **"advanced, novel or emerging technology"** material-risk duty (explicit, from 10 Jun 2026); IEC 62443-3-3 SL zoning; ISO/IEC 27019.
3. **Third-party / cloud / vendor AI** → CIP-013 supply-chain plans + FERC Order 912 directives (US); SG Cybersecurity Act Part 3A third-party-owned CII + s14(1)(bb) supplier-system incident reporting; NIS2 Art 21(2)(d) supply-chain security; NCCS Art 33/35 procurement; AU CIRMP s8 frameworks + s8B MFA; Japan ESP Act pre-screening of critical equipment and outsourced maintenance (foreign-vendor review); Chile Ley 21.663 Art 7 (ANCI standards).
4. **Incident detection/response obligations with short clocks** (AI monitoring tools must feed these): NCCS 4h (EU electricity); NERC CIP-008-6 1h; Chile 3h/24h/72h; AU SOCI 12h/72h; NIS2 24h/72h; SG CII 2h (secondary); AU ransomware 72h; Japan ACD "promptly" + 30 days; ANP 90-day report.
5. **Market-sensitive AI (trading/bidding/dispatch recommendation)** → REMIT/CNMC (ES), MIBEL (PT), NER bidding rules/AER (AU), EA Code Part 13 (NZ), FERC/CFTC anti-manipulation (US), CCEE/ANEEL (BR), Coordinador/CNE (CL), CAMMESA (AR), EGC/JEPX (JP), EMC/EMA (SG). Flag any AI that generates or executes offers/bids as market-sensitive with audit trail requirements.
6. **Consumer/metering data AI (billing, demand response, churn)** → LGPD (BR), Ley 21.719 from 1 Dec 2026 (CL), Ley 25.326 (AR), GDPR (PT/ES), APPI (JP), PDPA (SG), Privacy Act 2020 (NZ), CDR + Privacy Act 1988 (AU), state laws (US).
