## Forks

Forks are welcome. A fork must be renamed before it is published as a
product: keep the `NOTICE` file, state that your work is derived from this
repository, and do not call the fork "NexGuide.AI …" or "… GovMap". A fork
that stays private or is clearly labelled as an unofficial development copy
("fork of ai-governance-compliance-as-code") needs no rename.

(`eu-ai-act`, `iso42001`, …), the YAML schema and the Rego package names are
